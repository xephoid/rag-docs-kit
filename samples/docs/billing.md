# Plans and Billing

Tidepool is licensed per seat. A seat is one named user who can sign in to any
workspace on your server.

## Plans

The **Starter** plan is free for up to 3 seats and includes community support.
The **Team** plan costs $8 per seat per month, billed annually, and adds email
support with a one business day response time. The **Business** plan costs $15
per seat per month and adds single sign-on, audit logs, and phone support.

## Changing Seats

Admins can add seats at any time from the **Billing** page; new seats are
prorated for the rest of the billing period. Removing seats takes effect at the
next renewal date, and unused seats are not refunded mid-term.

## Refunds

We offer a full refund within 30 days of your first paid purchase. After 30 days,
annual subscriptions are non-refundable, but you can cancel auto-renewal at any
time. To request a refund, email billing@tidepool.example with your license key.

## Invoices and Payment Methods

Invoices are emailed to the billing contact on each renewal and can be
downloaded as PDF from the **Billing** page. We accept major credit cards and,
for Business plans, payment by bank transfer against a net-30 invoice.
